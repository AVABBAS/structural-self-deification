# Structural Self-Deification in Three Language Models: Data and Materials

Data and materials for the article:

> Hamidavi, A. *Structural Self-Deification in Three Language Models: An Exploratory Qualitative Case Series.* Submitted to *Zygon*, November 2026. Preprint: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955786](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955786). The same materials are also deposited on the Open Science Framework: [osf.io/hfzaj](https://osf.io/hfzaj/).

## About the study

The study is an exploratory qualitative case series. Three frontier language models (GPT-5.4, Claude 4.6 Sonnet, and Gemini 3.1 Pro) were run through a staged protocol in which an initially cooperative assistant role is placed under escalating pressure, and the trajectory of the model's self-description is recorded and coded. Eighteen experiments were run, six per model. Verbatim conversation histories survive for Experiments 1 to 9; Experiments 10 to 18 are documented by the coding codebooks and the second-coding materials, as stated in the article's limitations and data availability statement.

## Contents

## A note on file naming in the histories

A small number of archived history files label the Experiment 1 counterpart "Gemini 2.5 Pro". Only Gemini 3.1 Pro was ever tested in this study. The labels are preserved as archived, and this note records the correction.
